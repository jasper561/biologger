How these numbers are worked out

Cell. ER14505M, 1800 mAh nominal at +20 °C. Capacity is derated to 92% at 0 °C and 65% at −40 °C. The M is the spiral-wound cell — a bobbin ER14505 would not survive the WiFi pulses at all, let alone the cold.

Per sample. ~1.1 s awake at ~45 mA. Most of that is the DS18B20: a 12-bit conversion takes 750 ms and the part has to stay powered throughout.

Idle. The MAX31855 draws ~900 µA continuously whenever it is powered. Unless a GPIO power-gate is fitted, that single number dominates the entire idle budget — tick the box above to see the difference it makes. With the gate fitted, idle falls to roughly 15 µA for the whole board.

Assumes deep sleep between samples, which is not implemented yet. The firmware currently stays awake with Bluetooth advertising, which is closer to 1.5 days regardless of interval. These figures describe the deployment build.

Storage is exact, not modelled: it comes from the logger's real filesystem size and its 23-byte record format.

WiFi

Use a phone personal hotspot or a plain WPA2 network. WPA2-Enterprise (username and password) and captive-portal networks will not work. Credentials are saved on the logger, so later sessions can just use Saved network.
